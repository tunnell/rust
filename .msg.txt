std: xous: saturate the TCP connect timeout instead of truncating it

sockaddr_to_buf encoded the connect timeout with duration.as_millis() as
u64, so any timeout longer than u64::MAX milliseconds wrapped to an
arbitrary wire value: Duration::MAX happens to land on exactly u64::MAX,
but nearby oversized values can truncate to a tiny timeout or to 0,
which the net server reads as "no timeout at all".

Convert with u64::try_from and saturate to u64::MAX on overflow, the
same saturate-into-the-wire-domain approach the unix backend applied to
its connect timeout in rust#112405. The encoding for Duration::MAX and
for every realistic timeout is unchanged; the companion net-server clamp
keeps any u64 wire value from overflowing smoltcp's microsecond domain.

Validated under Renode by the net-tests suite: the timeouts theme stays
green with this client, and tcp::tcp_connect_timeout_duration_max_ok
flips via the server-side clamp it pairs with.
